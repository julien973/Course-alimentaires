Related Protocols

  • Typical breakfast (≈ 700 kcal / 46 g protein): 3 slices wholemeal bread + 3 eggs + 2 slices ham + 400 ml milk.
  • Night-time anti-biscuit kit: bowl prepared in the fridge — 250 g fat-free fromage blanc + 1 banana (≈ 200 kcal / 20 g protein). A large glass of milk before bed (casein = slow digestion all night long).

  • Coconut milk: mandatory for red kidney beans and stewed peas (Caribbean cooking) — 1 can of 400 ml per batch (≈ 140 kcal per serving). Log it in a calorie tracker.
  • ⚠️ Crème fraîche: always the light 15% version (167 kcal/100 g instead of ~300 for the full-fat one). 2 tablespoons ≈ 50 kcal. To lighten a sauce: half light cream / half fat-free fromage blanc.
  • Sweet potato: a rice substitute (1 portion = ~150 kcal for 175 g cooked) — to vary the plates.
  • Typical plate (≈ 800 kcal / 75 g protein): 150 g cooked rice + 250 g red kidney beans + 250 g chicken breast + 250 g volume vegetables.
  • ⚠️ Cooking oil: weigh it in your tracker — max 1 tablespoon per dish (90 kcal).
